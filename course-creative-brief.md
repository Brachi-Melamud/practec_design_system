# Practec DevOps Bootcamp + FDE Practitioner
## Program proposal, creative direction and production prompts

### The course theme

**From software developer to engineer who brings solutions to life.**

Hebrew campaign line: **לא רק לפתח. להביא פתרונות לפרודקשן.**

Practec offers experienced women developers two separate courses. **DevOps Bootcamp** (8 weeks) builds production-grade engineering and DevOps skills; **FDE Practitioner** (6 weeks) takes a team's project to production for a real client, the way a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) works. Together they tell one story: connecting business needs to working systems—understand the problem, design the solution, build it with AI, deploy it, and prepare it for production.

This is a selective, hands-on professional training program—not a promise of a job or guaranteed placement. Keep the tone ambitious, grounded, technically credible, and welcoming. Speak directly to technically strong women developers without suggesting they need to start their careers over.

### Message hierarchy

1. **Core promise:** Expand your software engineering skill set to design, build, and deploy real-world AI-enabled solutions.
2. **Courses:** two separately enrolled courses, 14 weeks in total—DevOps Bootcamp (8 weeks, ₪4,500, opens Sunday, 13 December 2026) and FDE Practitioner (6 weeks, ₪4,500, follows the bootcamp). Every week combines a 2.5-hour lecture with 6 hours of team work on the team's primary project. Up to 20 selected participants.
3. **Syllabus:** DevOps Bootcamp—production-grade engineering, Linux and networking, Git and collaboration with AI, containers, CI/CD, cloud, infrastructure as code, Kubernetes. FDE Practitioner—observability and reliability, DevSecOps, AI engineering, and Forward Deployed Engineering with delivery to real clients. See [Syllabus](#syllabus).
4. **Additional value:** Career-readiness support; optional two-week AIOps continuation.
5. **Admissions:** DevOps Bootcamp has a screening stage before the course—the practical screening assignment is published on Sunday, 29 November 2026, and candidates have one week to practice, revise, and submit it by Sunday, 6 December. The selection process also includes a professional assessment day (date not yet set—see [For review](#for-review-conflicts-with-existing-commitments)). FDE Practitioner has its own enrollment, open to DevOps Bootcamp graduates and to developers with equivalent DevOps experience.
6. **Expectation-setting:** Career support and relevant opportunity connections are offered, but employment or placement is not guaranteed.

## Syllabus

Two separate courses, 14 weeks in total, each numbered from week 1. Every week combines a 2.5-hour lecture with 6 hours of team work on the team's primary project.

- **The primary project.** Each team launches it in DevOps Bootcamp week 3—ideally from the team's own idea, with one or two placeholder projects available. It must integrate external APIs, it is containerized, deployed, secured, and AI-enabled along the way, and in FDE Practitioner it is delivered to a real client by a deadline.
- **Team roles.** Every team has developers and one responsible DevOps engineer for its primary project.
- **Cost-effective practice.** One primary project per team runs through both courses, and a shared practice (dummy) project carries the CI/CD and Kubernetes exercises, so environments are not rebuilt between weeks.

### Course 1 — DevOps Bootcamp (8 weeks, ₪4,500)

| Week | Module | Topics | Practice |

Ideation and process presentation: project ideas must include integration with external APIs; prepares the Git and collaboration stage (Brachi). |
| 2 | Linux and networking | Shell, processes and permissions, systemd, DNS, HTTP/TLS, reverse proxies and load balancers, troubleshooting tools | Wireshark, Packet Tracer, and course exercises. |
| 3 | Git, collaboration, and AI-assisted development | Branching strategies, trunk-based development, pull requests, semantic versioning, senior code review after peer review; prompt engineering, context, structured outputs, tool calling and agents, custom skills, tools, MCP | Launch the primary project—ideally self-initiated ideas, with one or two placeholder projects. Role: developer. |
| 4 | Containers | Docker, multi-stage builds, image hardening, Docker Compose, registries, Redis, JFrog Artifactory | Containerize the primary project and push it to a remote registry. Role: DevOps. |
| 5 | CI/CD | GitHub Actions, pipelines as code, quality gates, release strategies, GitOps | Implement on the practice project; one responsible DevOps engineer per primary project. |
| 6 | Cloud | AWS, IAM, VPC, virtual machines | Deploy to a VM in interactive remote sessions over SSH and the terminal. |

| 8 | Kubernetes | Workloads, services and ingress, configuration and secrets, Helm, autoscaling, RBAC, Argo CD | Run the practice project locally and in the cloud. |

6. **Observability practice.** The syllabus leaves this practice empty.

8. **Real clients.** FDE Practitioner commits to delivery for real clients; the clients are not yet identified.

11. **Proposed timetable and weekly agenda.** The syllabus PDFs (`syllabus/`) show a planned timetable—lecture Sunday 17:00–19:30, supervised team session Wednesday 17:00–21:00, plus 2 hours of independent team work—and a timed agenda, deliverable, and "done when" check for every week.
